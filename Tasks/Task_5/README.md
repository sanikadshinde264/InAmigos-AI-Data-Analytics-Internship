
# 🚀 AI Insights for NGO Growth

**Task 5 | InAmigos Foundation**

A short research project identifying **5 practical, AI-powered strategies** that can help an NGO improve its outreach, volunteer engagement, fundraising and online presence.

---

## 📌 Objective

To encourage analytical thinking and generate practical ideas that can benefit social organizations, using AI tools to research successful NGO practices and shape recommendations.

## 📄 Deliverable

The full write-up is in this repository:

- 📘 [`AI_Insights_for_NGO_Growth.docx`](./AI_Insights_for_NGO_Growth.docx)

## 💡 The 5 Strategies

| # | Strategy | Focus Area | Summary |
|---|----------|------------|---------|
| 1 | **Personalised Donor Communication** | Fundraising | Segment donors and use AI to draft tailored thank-yous, updates and appeals. |
| 2 | **Smart Volunteer Matching & Retention** | Volunteer engagement | Match volunteers to roles by skills and availability, and spot early signs of disengagement. |
| 3 | **Consistent Social Media Content Engine** | Online presence | Plan a month of content at once and turn one story into posts for multiple platforms. |
| 4 | **AI-Assisted Grant & CSR Proposal Writing** | Funding | Use AI for first drafts and funder matching, with humans verifying facts and budgets. |
| 5 | **Impact Storytelling with Data** | Outreach & trust | Turn programme data into simple dashboards, infographics and plain-language reports. |

## 🛠️ Tools Suggested

- **AI assistants:** ChatGPT, Claude
- **Design & scheduling:** Canva, Buffer, Meta Business Suite
- **Email:** Mailchimp, Brevo
- **Data & reporting:** Google Sheets, Excel, Looker Studio
- **Volunteer platforms:** Google Forms, Golden, VolunteerMatch

## ⚖️ Responsible AI Tips

- Never paste personal donor or beneficiary details into public AI tools.
- Always have a human review AI-generated content.
- Use real photos and real stories; never invent statistics.
- Start small, measure results, then expand.
